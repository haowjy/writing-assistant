# Find a reference answer for a writing task

This index links to 20 first-turn demonstrations of how the agent could answer writing, revision, planning and knowledge-base tasks. The answers were authored by an assistant for human review; they are not independently human-authored reference data.

The examples use decisions, interrupted tasks and dialogue to move 14 dramatised scenes forward. Three responses proposing alternatives and three continuity wikis apply the same restraint to planning and documentation. All five task families appear, with nine deliveries in the chat reply and eleven in files. The five loose briefs, which leave creative choices open, receive stated assumptions rather than silent choices. The scenes use different opening clauses and images, and every explicit word budget is met.

The family codes identify the kind of work: F1 is a scene in the reply; F2 is local revision or exploration followed by writing; F3 is alternatives for where the story could go; F4 is knowledge-base construction or updating; and F5 is writing supported by retrieved project facts. A knowledge base (KB) is the set of project reference files that records facts the writing must respect. A case ID such as F1-01 combines a family with an individual case number. “Channel” tells you whether the answer appears in a reply or a file.

Two scope limits matter when using the examples. F4-04 deliberately leaves two required final-state checks unmet because the accepted revision arrives in a later follow-up. F3-02 also answers only the first brief. The [verification report](../../training-data/worked-examples-report.md) records all results and limitations.
