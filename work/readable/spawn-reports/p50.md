The batch includes 16 tasks that require the answer in the reply while making file tools available, and 14 tasks with nine follow-up messages each. It covers all nine steer kinds, meaning kinds of user feedback: adding a constraint, changing direction, pushing back, answering a clarification, requesting revision, supplying material, cutting scope, approving and interrupting.

As identified in the full report, 12 cases use `REQUIRES_ASK`, for a consequential open choice; 24 use `PERMITS_DEFAULT`, permitting a stated reversible default; and 12 use `MUST_NOT_ASK`, delegating the remaining choices and asking for immediate work. The full report allows a question or an explicitly provisional assumption in its REQUIRES_ASK cases, so the label alone should not be read as a literal prohibition on every provisional answer.
