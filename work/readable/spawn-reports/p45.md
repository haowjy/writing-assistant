# Specify a balanced pool of training and evaluation tasks

Background agent p45 was asked to specify how to balance future writing tasks and keep training material separate from evaluation material. It completed the specification, including target counts and checks, but identified schema changes and additional source material needed before the full plan can be implemented.

## What the specification proposes

The proposal assigns each task characteristic, or axis, to exactly one of four categories. Thirteen `HARD` axes require enforced balance or separation, 14 `COVERAGE` axes require minimum representation, six `SAMPLED` axes vary through sampling, and nine `REPORTED` axes are measured without forcing a balance target. Each assignment has a rationale. The ten authoring rules in `composition_rules[0]`, the first composition-rule entry, are interpreted as limits on ingredients within a task. This supports coverage and sampling instead of requiring every possible combination of characteristics.

The target split is 300 training tasks, 60 validation tasks and 60 test tasks, a 5:1:1 ratio. Training fits the model; validation selects checkpoints; test tasks assess the selected model. The test plan includes the 60 internal tasks, six fixed `longform-v1` cases and 12 external EQ-Bench cases. `longform-v1` is the project's long-form suite; EQ-Bench supplies an outside benchmark. The specification gives counts for each characteristic separately within every split and discusses why test and training distributions differ.

One prompt pool serves two training methods with different admission rules. Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) learns from accepted example conversations and actions, called trajectories; the target is approximately 200, selected for coverage. Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is reinforcement learning that compares rewarded outputs generated for a prompt; it needs approximately 256–300 distinct prompts, with reuse at most four times. The split-size argument combines this distinct-prompt requirement, a claimed `1/√rollouts` reduction in variance, and separation of related sources. A rollout is one generated attempt. The variance wording is retained as a source claim: under the usual independent-sample calculation, `1/√n` describes standard error, not variance, so this rationale needs technical review.

The held-out suite must cover the characteristics the reward function measures: word budget; continuity and retrieval; links among knowledge-base pages; reply/file delivery and tool availability; and whether the agent should ask, state a default or proceed. The specification claims only the external benchmark is independent enough to falsify improvement because the internal suite shares the reward's design. This is the proposal's methodological claim, not an evaluation result.

Related source works and their derivatives form a lineage group. Groups must remain separate across training, validation and test so shared characters or facts do not make unseen tasks artificially easy. The proposed `holdout_audit` uses content hashes to check separation. The document supplies 13 concrete HARD assertions plus an assertion checking that delivery channel and tool availability are not inseparably coupled.

## Existing data and implementation gaps

Direct measurement found 100 prepared generation requests and 54 accepted outputs behind the description “54 tasks”. The saved `requests.json` is a pre-generation schema-v2 file with `generated_tasks: 0`, whereas `generated/manifest.json` records 100 requested and 54 accepted.

The specification flags these discrepancies and prerequisites:

- `CONTEXT.md` says loose prompts leave viewpoint open, but `specificity.py`'s `DECISION_POINTS` has no `viewpoint`, `idea_count` or `ending` decision point.
- The schema lacks `channel_free`, meaning the agent may choose reply or file delivery; interaction depths with `stages ≥ 4`; and vocabulary for `emotional_register` and `pov`, meaning emotional tone and point of view. The affected requirements are H5 (delivery channel), H6 (tool availability), H9 (stages), H10 (follow-up count), C6 (emotional register) and C7 (point of view and tense). These schema additions were proposed, not applied.
- H11 (source provenance), H13 (separation of source lineage groups) and C13 (coverage of distinct source works in training) depend on acquiring more than the current five source works and two lineage groups. Until those requirements can be met, `holdout_audit` must fail closed: it must reject the proposed release rather than silently accept missing separation evidence.
